# MASTER PROMPT — "Talking Toddler × Mommy/Daddy" Funny Moments Generator
For Facebook · AI video generation (Seedance / Veo / Kling / Higgsfield / similar) · 10s · 9:16 · U.S. audience

## How to use it
1. Paste everything in the code block below into any AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok — model-agnostic).
2. It gives you 10 fresh, random scenario ideas across three joke lanes: **Innocent Question**, **Honest Toddler**, and **Little Copycat**.
3. Pick a number, or ask for 10 fresh ones.
4. It confirms the child and the parent (and asks for a reference image if you have one).
5. It asks for the setting and the camera style.
6. It outputs the final generation prompt in four blocks: the video prompt (with exact spoken dialogue), the JSON version, posting notes, and a ready-to-post title + hashtag set.

**Reference video note:** your reference shows five different clips, each one a toddler chatting with Mommy or Daddy and landing a funny line. This master prompt copies that *look and feel* only. It generates ONE standalone 10-second clip at a time. No text layers, no rankings, no compilation, no captions.

## Why it's built this way

- **Built from your reference, minus the editing.** The five clips in your video share one formula: toddler + Mommy/Daddy + warm American living room + a single punchline + the parent's reaction. The ranking layers, captions, and compilation format are deliberately left out. Each generation is one clean 10-second clip you can post on its own or edit later.
- **Three lanes, matched to what the reference clips actually do.** Lane 1 is the belly / "you ate a baby?!" misunderstanding. Lane 2 is the "is Daddy rich? then why did you marry him?" honest-roast style. Lane 3 is the copycat and mischief clips (the fake belly with hands on hips, the stickers on Daddy's face). Splitting them means you always get a mix, and you can say "give me 10 Honest Toddler only" whenever one lane is hot.
- **Dialogue is the new core, so it has its own rules.** Your Baby × Animal prompt used non-word baby sounds. This niche needs real spoken lines, and AI video tools handle short lines far better than long ones. That's why the prompt caps total words at 25, child lines at 8 words, and forces the first line inside 1.5 seconds. Every line is written out word for word so the generator doesn't invent its own.
- **Cause-before-reaction, kept from your original.** The child's punchline lands first, and only then does the parent's face change and the laugh start. Without this, the reaction happens on top of the line and the joke dies.
- **Randomness is built into six axes.** Child age and look, parent, topic, punchline mechanic, setting, and camera style are all rotated for every idea, with a rule against defaulting to the most obvious joke. That's what keeps a page from posting the same belly joke ten times.
- **A clean-humor rule protects the page.** Some lines in the reference lean toward adult innuendo. Those are blocked here: no adult double meanings in a child's mouth, no body jokes, no mean parents. That keeps the content shareable by grandparents and safer for Facebook reach and for the AI tool's content filters.
- **American audience is locked into the casting, voice, and references.** Home architecture, clothing, parent speech ("oh my gosh," "buddy," "wait, say that again"), and everyday topics (pizza night, Santa, tooth fairy, school drop-off) all read as American. One honest limit: video generators control accent unevenly, so if a voice sounds off, regenerate or try a different tool. Appearance and setting are far more reliable than accent.
- **Block C adds a Facebook-specific posting layer.** The caption hook, pinned comment question, and AI-label reminder are there because performance on this kind of content depends heavily on "tag a mom/dad" comments and on staying within Meta's AI-disclosure rules.
